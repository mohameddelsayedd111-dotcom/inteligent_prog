---

## Overview

This report compares the performance of two approaches implemented for heart disease detection:
1. **Rule-Based Expert System** (Experta) — 12 clinical rules
2. **Decision Tree Classifier** (Scikit-Learn) — trained on 303 patient records

---

## Key Observations

### Expert System
- **Strengths:** Fully transparent, no training data required, clinically interpretable,
  easy to audit and extend by domain experts.
- **Weaknesses:** Limited to pre-defined rules, cannot learn from new patterns,
  may miss complex feature interactions.

### Decision Tree
- **Strengths:** Learns from data, captures non-linear interactions, higher accuracy,
  generates feature importance rankings.
- **Weaknesses:** Can overfit without pruning, requires labeled data, less interpretable
  to non-technical clinicians.

---

## Recommendation

A **hybrid approach** is recommended for clinical deployment:
- Use the **Expert System** as a first-pass screening tool (transparent, auditable).
- Use the **Decision Tree** for a secondary quantitative assessment.
- Flag patients where both systems agree on HIGH risk for immediate clinical review.
